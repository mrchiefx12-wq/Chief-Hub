01 I cannot create or access my CHIEF account +
Solution

First, check that your internet connection is working correctly and make sure all account information has been entered accurately. 02 I forgot my password or cannot sign in +
Solution

Check that your email address and password are entered correctly and that there are no unnecessary spaces. If a password recovery option is available, use it to restore access to your account. Never share your password with anyone. If you are still unable to sign in, contact CHIEF Support for further assistance.

03 My product verification code is not working +
Solution

Carefully check every character of your verification code. Make sure there are no extra spaces or typing mistakes. Enter the code exactly as provided. 04 My daily reward has not appeared +
Solution

Refresh the application or page and check again after a short time. Some rewards may require processing before they appear in your account. Avoid repeatedly claiming the same reward. If the reward remains missing after the required time, contact CHIEF Support and explain when and how you completed the activity.

05 My C Coins or reward balance is incorrect +
Solution

First, refresh your account and allow the system a short time to update your balance. Check whether the activity was successfully completed. If your balance still appears incorrect, contact support and provide a clear description of the activity and the issue you noticed. Do not repeatedly perform the same action.

06 My referral reward has not been added +
Solution

Referral rewards may depend on the referred user completing the required steps. Check that the referral process was completed correctly. If all required conditions were completed but the reward is still missing, contact CHIEF Support so the referral can be reviewed.

07 Reward ads are not loading or not giving rewards +
Solution

Check your internet connection and try again after a short time. Advertisement availability can sometimes vary depending on the current network or ad availability. Do not repeatedly click the reward option. If the issue continues, contact CHIEF Support and mention what happened.

08 My voucher is not working or cannot be used +
Solution

Check that the voucher information has been entered correctly and confirm that the voucher is valid for the intended use. Keep your voucher information private and do not share it publicly. If the voucher should be working but is not accepted, contact CHIEF Support with the relevant information.

09 My order or custom request has not been updated +
Solution

Some orders and requests may require time for review and processing. Please allow reasonable time for your request to be handled. If you have waited and still have no update, contact CHIEF Support with your order or request information so the team can assist you.

10 A page, feature, or button is not working +
Solution

Check your internet connection first. Refresh the page or completely close and reopen the application. If the feature still does not work, try again after a short time. When contacting support, mention the exact page, feature, or button that is causing the problem.

11 The application or WebView is showing a blank screen +
Solution

Check your internet connection and restart the application. If the problem continues, close the app completely and open it again. Make sure your device has a stable connection. If the blank screen remains, contact CHIEF Support and mention which page was showing the issue.
